Kia Ora Parents and Students,


John Paul College (JPC) is a Catholic co-educational secondary school located in Rotorua, New Zealand, serving students from Year 7 to Year 13. JPC is committed to academic excellence, spiritual growth, cultural enrichment, and sporting achievement. The college provides a supportive and inclusive environment, encouraging students to reach their full potential while living out Christian values. With a strong focus on faith, service, and community, JPC continues to foster young people who are confident, compassionate, and prepared to contribute positively to society.


JPC offers a wide range of academic, cultural, sporting, and leadership opportunities, ensuring every student can discover and develop their strengths. The college embraces diversity and celebrates the unique gifts of each individual, while maintaining high expectations for learning and behaviour. With dedicated teachers, modern facilities, and a vibrant school community, John Paul College continues to be a place where students are inspired to grow in knowledge, faith, and character.


We are located at Whitworth Road, Utuhina, Rotorua
